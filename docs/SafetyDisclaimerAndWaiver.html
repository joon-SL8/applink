2. Exercise Risks

Physical exercise and cycling involve inherent risks, including:

  • Muscle and joint injuries;
  • Overexertion;
  • Falls;
  • Dehydration;
  • Heat-related illness;
  • Dizziness;
  • Fainting;
  • Cardiovascular events;
  • Abnormal heart rhythms;
  • Loss of coordination;
  • Equipment-related injuries; and
  • Other serious injury, illness, or medical events.

You voluntarily assume the ordinary risks associated with physical exercise and use of a cycling trainer, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

3. ERG Mode and Automatic Resistance

ERG mode and other automatic workout modes may cause your trainer to automatically change resistance.

Resistance may increase substantially when your cadence decreases.

You should not attempt to maintain a programmed power target if doing so causes excessive or unsafe physical effort.

If resistance becomes excessive or unexpected:

  1. Reduce or stop pedaling as appropriate;
  2. Reduce the workout intensity or exit ERG mode;
  3. Use the trainer's manual controls or emergency procedure if necessary; and
  4. Stop the workout if you cannot safely continue.

Do not rely on the App as the sole means of stopping a trainer in an emergency.

The trainer may behave differently depending on its hardware, firmware, calibration, communication state, and manufacturer design.

4. FTP and Workout Targets

The App may use FTP to calculate workout intensity.

FTP is a cycling performance metric. It is not a measure of medical fitness, cardiovascular health, or exercise safety.

Your FTP may be inaccurate or outdated.

Workout targets based on FTP may therefore be inappropriate for your current physical condition.

Do not attempt to reach or maintain a target merely because the App indicates that you should be capable of doing so.

You are always permitted to reduce the intensity or stop the workout.

5. FTP Tests and Maximal Efforts

FTP tests, ramp tests, maximal-effort workouts, and other high-intensity workouts may require substantial or near-maximal physical effort.

Before participating in such workouts, consider whether you are physically prepared for strenuous exercise.

If you have concerns about your ability to perform high-intensity exercise, consult an appropriate healthcare professional before participating.

An FTP test result is a training metric only. It is not a medical assessment.

6. Stop Exercise When You Feel Abnormal

Stop exercising immediately if you experience unusual, severe, or concerning symptoms.

Examples include:

  • Chest pain, pressure, or discomfort;
  • Severe or unusual shortness of breath;
  • Fainting or feeling that you may faint;
  • Severe dizziness;
  • Confusion;
  • Unusual or irregular heartbeat or palpitations;
  • Sudden weakness or loss of coordination;
  • Severe or unexpected pain; or
  • Any other symptom that feels abnormal or concerning.

If symptoms are severe, persistent, or potentially life-threatening, seek appropriate medical attention immediately.

Do not rely on the App, trainer, heart-rate monitor, power meter, smartwatch, or another connected device to determine whether a symptom is medically significant.

7. Consult a Healthcare Professional

Before beginning a new exercise program or performing high-intensity or maximal-effort workouts, consider consulting a qualified healthcare professional if you have concerns about your ability to exercise safely.

This is particularly important if you have a medical condition, cardiovascular risk factors, are taking medication that may affect exercise performance, or have otherwise been advised to limit physical activity.

8. Equipment Safety

Before each workout:

  • Confirm that your bicycle is correctly installed and secured;
  • Confirm that the trainer is correctly installed;
  • Follow the trainer manufacturer's instructions;
  • Perform required calibration;
  • Inspect equipment for damage;
  • Confirm that the trainer is functioning normally;
  • Ensure adequate ventilation and cooling;
  • Have adequate hydration available; and
  • Keep children, pets, and other persons away from moving equipment.

Do not use damaged or improperly installed equipment.

9. Environmental Conditions

Indoor cycling can produce substantial heat, sweating, dehydration, and fatigue.

Exercise in an appropriate environment with adequate ventilation and cooling.

Maintain appropriate hydration and take breaks when necessary.

Do not exercise when illness, fatigue, dehydration, medication, alcohol, or another condition may impair your ability to exercise safely.

Communication may fail or be interrupted because of:

The App cannot guarantee uninterrupted communication or accurate trainer response.

Power, cadence, speed, heart-rate, FTP, resistance, training-load, and other measurements may be affected by sensor accuracy, trainer calibration, hardware limitations, software calculations, firmware, environmental conditions, or user-provided information.

Do not treat training data as medically validated measurements.
